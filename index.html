About us

Meaningful technology, carefully built.

Mythreyi Information Technology Private Limited is a technology company registered in Andhra Pradesh, India, in 2022. We design and build digital products that are simple to use, respectful of people's privacy and made to last.

Our first product, Vijayam Classifieds, brings local property and job listings online in English and Telugu, so that people can use it comfortably in their own language, on any phone.

Our approach

Four steps from idea to impact

A clear, shared process, so that you always know where your project stands.

  1. Step 1

    Discover

    We listen first: to the people, the problem and the constraints, and write them down before any code is written.

  2. Step 2

    Design

    We shape the screens, the data and the architecture, and review them with you while changes are still easy.

  3. Step 3

    Develop

    We build in small, tested steps, with security and documentation included from the start.

  4. Step 4

    Deliver

    We release carefully, then monitor, back up and keep improving. Delivery is the start of the service, not the end.
